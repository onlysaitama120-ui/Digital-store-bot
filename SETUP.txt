=====================================================
   HOW THE ORDER SYSTEM WORKS
=====================================================

OPTION A - Normal order (ticket):
1. Buyer clicks "Place Order" button in #create-ticket
2. Fills in: Product, Quantity, Offer, Notes
3. Bot creates a private ticket: order-1, order-2, etc.
4. Staff discusses payment with buyer in the ticket
5. Staff clicks "Mark Complete" when done
6. Buyer gets a "⭐ Vouch Now" button
7. Buyer clicks it, writes a review
8. Vouch posts in #vouches AND order log in #orders

OPTION B - FAST vouch (just type in #vouches):
1. Buyer types in #vouches:
   +legit bought potato in 20 rupees
   +rep bought 1x Nitro BOOST fw [450% up]
   +trusted bought pc parts in 1500 rupees
   vouch bought game account for $5
2. Bot detects it AUTOMATICALLY
3. Bot creates "✅ ORDER COMPLETED" box in #orders:
     Order No: #12
     Item: Potato
     Price: INR 20
     Buyer: @buyer
4. Bot replies with a "⭐ Vouch Logged" confirmation
5. Bot saves the vouch for the seller

FORMATS THAT WORK:
  +legit bought [ITEM] in [PRICE] rupees
  +rep [SELLER-ID] legit bought [ITEM] fw [TAG]
  bought [ITEM] for $[PRICE]
  vouch bought [ITEM] for [PRICE]rs
  +trusted bought [ITEM] in ₹[PRICE]

The bot auto-detects: product name, price (rs/rupees/inr/$/usd),
and seller (from mention or 17-20 digit ID).
